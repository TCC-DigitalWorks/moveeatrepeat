Delete your account and data

You can delete your Move. Eat. Repeat. account and data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Privacy, export & delete data.
  3. Tap Delete account & all data and confirm.

This deletes everything on your phone straight away and, if you’re signed in, your account and backed-up data on our servers.

If you no longer have the app

Email tcc.digitalworks@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”, from the email address you signed in with. We’ll confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your profile and preferences, including diet, allergies and limitations
  • Your workout history, meal plans and shopping lists

What we keep

Purchase records needed for tax and accounting, kept for up to 6 years as the law requires. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

Cancelling Premium: deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
