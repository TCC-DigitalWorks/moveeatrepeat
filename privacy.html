Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

This policy explains what the Move. Eat. Repeat. app (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is run by TCC Digital Works. You can contact us at tcc.digitalworks@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can use the app without an account. Then everything stays on your phone.
  • If you sign in, we back up your profile, workout history and shopping lists so you can restore them on a new phone.
  • Your daily check-ins, weight, water and anything from Health Connect stay on your phone.
  • We never sell your data and there are no adverts.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time in the app.

Health Connect (Samsung Health, Google Fit)

If you choose to connect, the app reads your steps and weight and writes the workouts you finish. Steps are shown on your Home screen; weigh-ins are added to the weight log on your phone. We only read this data while you have the app open, we don’t send it to our servers, and we never use it for advertising or share it with anyone. The use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can remove access at any time in Health Connect settings.

Our legal basis (UK GDPR)

We use your data to provide the app you asked for (contract). Information such as diet, allergies, movement limitations and health readings can be special category health data; we only use it with your explicit consent, which you give by entering it or connecting Health Connect, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting it in the app. Product analytics are off unless you turn them on.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and storage, hosted in London, UK.
  • Google: “Continue with Google” sign-in, and Google Play for purchases.
  • RevenueCat (when subscriptions launch): manages subscription status. It receives an anonymous app user ID, not your health data.

We don’t sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

How long we keep it

We keep your backed-up data while you have an account. When you delete your account we delete it from our servers within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example, purchase records for tax).

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Most of this is in the app (Profile → Privacy, export & delete data). Or email tcc.digitalworks@gmail.com. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children

The app is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If we make important changes we’ll tell you in the app before they apply.
